SIGNAL METHODOLOGY

A transparent
valuation framework.

Implied value is a comparative estimate for pre-token protocols, not a prediction of launch price or guaranteed return. Missing 7D growth defaults to 0%; missing listing age uses 365 days. Listing age is a maturity proxy, not verified protocol age.

MODEL FORMULATVL × 0.35 × (1 + clamp(7D growth, −50%, 100%) / 200) × (0.65 + 0.35 × min(listing age, 1095) / 1095)
01Base multipleStarting valuation anchor for the protocol.
02Growth multiplierReflects recent protocol growth signals.
03Maturity multiplierAdjusts for protocol maturity and launch context.

Estimates are directional and depend on the supplied model inputs. They are not financial advice.
